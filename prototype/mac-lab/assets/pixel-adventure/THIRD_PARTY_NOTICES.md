# Pixel Adventure artwork by Pixel Frog

Character artwork, animation strips, background tiles, and terrain are by **Pixel Frog**.

- Author's page: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/pixel-adventure-1
- Author's original upload: https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-adventure-1
- Downloaded original archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Pixel%20Adventure%201.zip
- Current author-declared license: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The older OpenGameArt upload is also identified as **CC BY 4.0** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Sources checked: 2026-09-08.

The shipped PNG bytes are unchanged. Files have been selected and renamed into
`pink`, `frog`, and `world` directories. Original character names are **Pink Man**
and **Ninja Frog**. The original character sheets have 32 × 32 pixel frames.
`asset-hashes.json` records the shipped PNG SHA-256 values.

PocketPal's CSS/JavaScript plays the original strips and adds movement, turning,
simple reaction symbols, and attachment coordinates. The original character art
was not redrawn or recolored. The animated preview is an export of the original
frames with layout, timing, and movement supplied by PocketPal.

No original sleeping, eating, speaking, or arm-waving animation is claimed.
The “rest” action slows the idle cycle. Greeting and petting use the original
idle pose with movement and a reaction symbol.
